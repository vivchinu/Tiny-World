🥽 Meta Quest 3 Testing Guide

Option A — Wi-Fi (Direct in Meta Quest Browser):

Ensure your Quest 3 is on the same Wi-Fi network, open the Meta Quest Browser, and navigate to:

https://10.45.194.197:3001

*Tap 'Advanced' → 'Proceed' when prompted about the local self-signed certificate.

Option B — USB Cable (ADB Port Forwarding):

If using Quest Link or USB cable with ADB:

adb reverse tcp:3000 tcp:3000

Then open: http://localhost:3000

Meta Quest 3 Direct Hand Tracking Gestures:
  • Middle Finger Pinch (Rotate & Zoom): Pinch your middle finger to your thumb. Move horizontally to rotate the terrain, or move vertically to zoom / scale up & down. Two-handed middle pinch allows holographic scaling!
  • Index Pinch (Draw / Place / Click): Pinch your index finger to your thumb to draw houses, walls, paths, and place trees/props. Also tap buttons on the 3D palette.
  • Forward-Render Hand Skeleton: Holographic 3D finger bones and highlighted joints are forward-rendered so your hands are always visible in front of the island.
  • Left Palm Glance (3D Tool Palette): Turn your left palm towards your face to open the floating tool menu. Turn it away to dismiss.
  • Touch Controllers (Alternative): Right Trigger to draw, Left Palm / Point to select tools, Right Thumbstick to rotate & zoom, Grip buttons to reposition island.
